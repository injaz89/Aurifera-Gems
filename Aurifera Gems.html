Welcome to Aurifera Gems – where elegance meets the earth's finest treasures. Founded on January 2, 2022, Aurifera Gems is a premier provider of exquisite and ethically sourced gemstones.

Located in the heart of Sri Lanka, a land renowned for its rich gem heritage, we pride ourselves on delivering handpicked, high-quality stones for collectors, jewelers, and gem enthusiasts across the globe.

Our mission is to preserve the legacy of fine gem craftsmanship while ensuring sustainability and authenticity in every gem we offer. Whether you're seeking the perfect sapphire, ruby, or any rare gemstone, Aurifera Gems is your trusted destination for timeless brilliance.
